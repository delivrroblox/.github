## About

We're building Delivr to help Roblox creators sell and deliver products through their own customizable hubs. It connects with Discord to handle purchases, verification, and product access without having to do everything manually.

We're still in development. Discord integration is live, with more features on the way.

## Features

Here's what we're building:

- Customizable hubs for your products and community
- Automatic product delivery and whitelisting
- Roblox account linking and Discord role sync
- Limiteds, subscriptions, and discount codes
- Product transfers, gifts, and loans
- Team permissions, blacklists, and alerts
- API access for your own tools

We also have a web dashboard, custom bot hosting, purchase webhooks, and migration from Parcel, Vault, and myPod / Eviglo planned.

## Free and Pro

The free plan includes 3 hubs, 100 products per hub, full customization, and 200 API calls.

Pro is a one-time purchase with lifetime access. It increases the limit to 20 hubs and includes unlimited products and API calls. Custom webstore domains are coming later.

Custom bot hosting will be a separate monthly add-on. Check the [website](https://delivrroblox.net/#pricing) for pricing and plan details.
